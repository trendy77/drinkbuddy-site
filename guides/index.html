Drink-driving limits & the law

Legal BAC limits around Australia and the world, breath testing and driving the morning after.

  • The drink driving limit in Australia
    The drink driving limit in Australia is 0.05 for full-licence drivers and zero for learners and P-platers. See the limits, penalties and how many drinks it takes.
  • Can I drive the morning after drinking?
    Slept it off? You could still be over 0.05 the morning after drinking. How your body clears alcohol overnight, the maths, and when it's safe to drive.
  • How do breathalysers work?
    How a breathalyser turns your breath into a BAC reading, the 2100:1 breath-to-blood ratio, fuel cell vs sensor devices, why nothing beats a test, and how RBT works in Australia.
  • Can you drink and drive a boat in Australia?
    Can you drink and drive a boat? In most of Australia the skipper must stay under 0.05 BAC — the same as the road. The state-by-state limits, on-water testing and risks.
  • What's the legal BAC limit for driving in the US?
    The drunk-driving limit is 0.08% BAC in every US state except Utah, where it's 0.05%. See the limits for commercial and under-21 drivers, and what 0.08 feels like.
  • What's the legal blood alcohol limit in Canada?
    Canada's criminal blood alcohol limit is 0.08, but most provinces penalize drivers from 0.05 — and Saskatchewan from 0.04. See the limits, fines and zero-tolerance rules.
  • What's the drink-driving limit in New Zealand?
    New Zealand's drink-driving limit is 250mcg of alcohol per litre of breath (50mg blood) for drivers 20+, and zero under 20. See the penalties at each level.
  • UK alcohol units explained: how many is too many?
    One UK unit is 10ml of pure alcohol — and a large glass of wine is three of them. How to count units, the 14-a-week guideline and drink-drive limits.
  • What's the drink-driving limit in the UK?
    The UK drink-driving limit is 80mg/100ml blood (0.08%) in England, Wales and NI, but 50mg (0.05%) in Scotland. The breath figures, the penalties, and what it means for Aussie drivers.
  • What's the drink-driving limit in Ireland?
    Ireland's drink-driving limit is 50mg of alcohol per 100ml of blood (0.05%) for experienced drivers, and 20mg for learners, novices and professional drivers.
  • What's the drink-driving limit in Germany?
    Germany's drink-driving limit is 0.5‰ (0.05% BAC) — but zero for new and professional drivers, and 0.3‰ can still be an offence. The limits, fines and traveller tips.
  • Drink-driving limits in Europe: a country-by-country guide
    Drink-driving limits across Europe by country: most are 0.05 like Australia, several are zero-tolerance, and the UK sits at 0.08. Here's the full guide for travellers.
  • What's the drink-driving limit in Japan?
    Japan's drink-driving limit is 0.03% BAC (0.15 mg/L breath) — one of the world's strictest. Penalties, passenger rules, and what it means for Aussie drivers.
  • What's the drink driving limit in Thailand?
    Thailand's drink-driving limit is 0.05%, dropping to 0.02% for newer drivers — with fines, jail and deportation at stake. What Aussie travellers must know.
  • What's the drink-driving limit in Dubai and the UAE?
    Dubai and the UAE have zero tolerance on drink-driving — any alcohol at all while driving can mean jail, a heavy fine, a one-year licence ban and deportation.
